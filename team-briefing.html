Why it's safe to start.
02 / THE GUARDRAILS
Documents, not people
No meeting recording. We work from artifacts, not behavior.
You release it
Nothing enters shared memory until its owner says so.
Access never widens
Restricted stays restricted. Sharing a doc never broadens who can see it.
Private stays private
Your own notes are yours. Contributing is always voluntary.
Everything is traceable
Every fact points back to its source and who released it.
Trust first. Capability follows.
