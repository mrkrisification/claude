# A1 Croatia

**Type:** Subsidiary of A1 Telekom Austria Group · **Country:** Croatia · **Sector:** Telecommunications
**Last updated:** 2026-06-11

## Overview
A1 Hrvatska d.o.o. (formerly Vipnet, rebranded 1 October 2018) is a converged mobile, fixed-line, broadband and TV operator in Croatia, and a wholly-owned subsidiary of A1 Telekom Austria Group (Vienna). A1 Telekom Austria Group is itself majority-owned by América Móvil (~60.8%), with ÖBAG (Austrian state holding, ~28.4%) and free float (~10.8%) making up the remainder (as of October 2025). A1 Croatia is the #2 operator in the Croatian telecom market behind Hrvatski Telekom (Deutsche Telekom subsidiary).

## Market position
A1 Croatia serves approximately 2.1 million customers across mobile, fixed-line and TV services (end-2024; ~2.0m end-2023, ~2.1m most recently reported for 2025), of which ~0.7 million are fixed-line RGUs (+0.1% YoY in 2024).

Mobile market share trend (HAKOM, official regulator data, by subscribers, out of ~4.7m total mobile subscribers in Croatia, +3.4% YoY):
- **End-2024**: HT 44.4% / **A1 34.1%** / Telemach 21.5%
- **Q1 2025 / 2025**: A1 ~33.8%–34.1% — consistent continuation of the end-2024 trend

The previously noted ~20% A1 share figure could not be traced to any HAKOM publication and is now considered erroneous/outdated; it has been discarded in favor of the HAKOM-confirmed ~34% figure.

Key competitors:
- **Hrvatski Telekom (HT)** — Deutsche Telekom subsidiary, market leader (~44.4% mobile share, end-2024 HAKOM data)
- **Telemach Croatia** — ~21.5% mobile share (end-2024); aggressive pricing, claims 98.9% population 5G coverage by end-2024
- **Tele2 Croatia** — smaller mobile operator

Sector trends: convergence (mobile + cable/fixed broadband bundling) is A1's key differentiator for retaining high-value households. FTTH passed ~409,000 households (2024), with over 672,000 premises connected to gigabit-capable broadband and >€200M annual fiber investment, targeting 70% household coverage. 5G covers 98% of Croatia; 5G Standalone is live in Zagreb, Split, Rijeka and key tourist routes; 5G Homebox (FWA) coverage reached ~92% by late 2025/early 2026. In the 2023 spectrum auction, A1 Hrvatska invested €109.3m vs HT's €135.3m and Telemach's €94.4m. HAKOM reports the overall Croatian electronic communications market generated €1.8bn revenue in 2024 (+growth), with €427m total operator investment (mobile portion €116.2m, +26% YoY). The Croatian telecom market overall is valued at ~$1.91bn (2025), projected to reach ~$2.18bn by 2030.

## Recent developments
- **June 2024** — Dejan Turk appointed CEO of A1 Croatia (and Slovenia), succeeding Jiri Dvorjancansky after 10+ years; remains CEO as of May 2026. (SeeNews, 2024-06; A1 Hrvatska leadership page)
- **2025** — Strategic partnership with SkyShowtime for streaming bundling; Sportklub returned to A1's TV offering on 1 December 2025 (free promo through 31 March 2026).
- **2025–2026** — Ongoing 3G network shutdown (started in Dubrovnik, continuing through 2026) as part of network modernization.
- **2025** — Launched A1 Security Operations Center; added Starlink to product offering. (A1 Hrvatska, 2025)
- **January 2026** — A1 Hrvatska (alongside HT) announced a price increase effective with March 2026 bills, applying an inflation-indexation clause of ~3.7% average annual inflation. (Glas Slavonije, 2026-01-26)
- **May 2026** — Ericsson Nikola Tesla signed a new multi-year (5-year) agreement to remain A1 Hrvatska's exclusive radio access network supplier and maintenance provider, as part of network modernization. (SeeNews / Točka Na I, 2026-05)
- **February 2026** — A1 Telekom Austria Group reported a "very solid" FY2025 with group revenue ~€5.58bn and EBITDA growth, driven by CEE operations despite a challenging Austrian market; group-level Croatia segment breakdown not yet retrieved. (MarketScreener, 2026-02; EQS News press release)
- A1 Group plans ~€750m group-wide 2026 investment (excluding spectrum) continuing fiber/5G rollout.
