# A1 Croatia

**Type:** Subsidiary of A1 Telekom Austria Group · **Country:** Croatia · **Sector:** Telecommunications
**Last updated:** 2026-06-11

## Overview
A1 Hrvatska d.o.o. (formerly Vipnet, rebranded 1 October 2018) is a converged mobile, fixed-line, broadband and TV operator in Croatia, and a wholly-owned subsidiary of A1 Telekom Austria Group (Vienna). A1 Telekom Austria Group is itself majority-owned by América Móvil (~60.8%), with ÖBAG (Austrian state holding, ~28.4%) and free float (~10.8%) making up the remainder (as of October 2025). A1 Croatia is generally regarded as the #2 operator in the Croatian telecom market behind Hrvatski Telekom.

## Market position
A1 Croatia serves approximately 2 million customers across mobile, fixed-line and TV services (2025). Mobile subscriber market share estimates conflict between sources: ~33.8% (Q1 2025, A1 ranked #2) vs. a broader 2025 estimate of HT ~46% / Telemach ~35% / A1 ~20% — these should be cross-checked against HAKOM's official quarterly market-share reports.

Key competitors:
- **Hrvatski Telekom (HT)** — Deutsche Telekom subsidiary, market leader (~46% mobile share by some estimates)
- **Telemach Croatia** — aggressive pricing, claims 98.9% population 5G coverage by end-2024
- **Tele2 Croatia** — smaller mobile operator

Sector trends: convergence (mobile + cable/fixed broadband bundling) is A1's key differentiator for retaining high-value households. FTTH passed ~409,000 households (2024), with over 672,000 premises connected to gigabit-capable broadband and >€200M annual fiber investment, targeting 70% household coverage. 5G covers 98% of Croatia; 5G Standalone is live in Zagreb, Split, Rijeka and key tourist routes. The Croatian telecom market is valued at ~$1.91bn (2025), projected to reach ~$2.18bn by 2030.

## Recent developments
- **June 2024** — Dejan Turk appointed CEO of A1 Croatia (and Slovenia), succeeding Jiri Dvorjancansky after 10+ years. (SeeNews, 2024-06)
- **2025** — Strategic partnership with SkyShowtime for streaming bundling. (Mobile Europe, 2025)
- **2025–2026** — Ongoing 3G network shutdown (started in Dubrovnik, continuing through 2026) as part of network modernization.
- **2025** — Launched A1 Security Operations Center; added Starlink to product offering. (A1 Hrvatska, 2025)
- **2026** — 5G Standalone network now covers Zagreb, Split, Rijeka, most islands, and key tourist routes. (Telecompaper)
- A1 Group plans ~€750m group-wide 2026 investment (excluding spectrum) continuing fiber/5G rollout, with strong CEE results in 2025 driving group growth. (Mobile Europe, 2025)
