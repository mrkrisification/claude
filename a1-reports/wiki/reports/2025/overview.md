# FY2025 — Record revenue; Belarus dividend restrictions persist

*Source: `2025_A1_AnnualFinancialReport_EN.pdf` (English). Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2025`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Record revenue €5,577 mn** (+3.0%) with EBITDA €2,062 mn; customer base expanded in both fixed-line and mobile. (DB, mgmt report)
- Continued **fibre expansion and 5G**; prior-year trends held in a challenging, competitive environment. (mgmt report)
- **Austria segment = 49% of group revenue** — CEE now slightly more than half, the long-running shift continued. (mgmt report)
- **Belarus dividend-payment restrictions in place since April 2024 remained effective** (geopolitical/sanctions context). (mgmt report)
- **Net result €613 mn.** (DB)

## Notes
- KPI reporting requirements continue to evolve (ARPU and others). The Belarus restriction is the main standing geopolitical overhang on the group's cash flows.
