# FY2012 — América Móvil takes a stake

*Source: `2012_TAG_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2012`).*

## Highlights & events
- **América Móvil (Carlos Slim's group) acquired a 22.76% stake** — a new strategic shareholder alongside the state holding ÖIAG (~28%). (mgmt report / shareholder structure)
- Differentiated multi-brand strategy across markets (e.g. the no-frills "bob"); Si.mobil meeting rising competition in Slovenia. (mgmt report)
- Revenue €4,329.7 mn (−2.8%); net result €103.8 mn. (DB)

## Notes
- The arrival of América Móvil sets up the 2014 control change — the second defining ownership event after the 2000 IPO.
