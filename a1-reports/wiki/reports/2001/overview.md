# FY2001 — CEE mobile expansion, UMTS build-out, wireline reorganisation

*Source: `2001_AnnualReport.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2001`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Mobilkom acquired a controlling stake in Si.mobil (Slovenia)** in February — an early step in the CEE mobile footprint. (p4)
- **UMTS system suppliers selected** (Ericsson, Nortel) for the first Austrian UMTS network. (p4)
- New **TikTak** fixed-line tariff family launched; **JV with SAP & Hewlett-Packard** for e-business solutions. (p4)
- **Increased stake in VIPnet (Croatia)**; further GSM 1800 MHz frequency acquisitions. (p4)
- Market leader in all four segments; ~16,600 employees at year-end (continued headcount reduction); total managed revenue ~€3.9 bn; market cap €4.7 bn. (p3)
- **Net loss of €−104.6 mn** (telecom-sector downturn, UMTS/restructuring costs). (DB)

## Notes
- Begins the CEE growth thread (Slovenia, Croatia) that defines the next two decades; Austrian core still under competitive/restructuring pressure.
