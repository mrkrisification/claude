# FY2020 — COVID-19; 5G launches in Austria

*Source: `2020_A1_TAG_Combined-Annual-Report_EN.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2020`).*

## Highlights & events
- **COVID-19 pandemic** dominated the year; the group proved **resilient** (roaming hit offset by data demand), with revenue broadly stable. (mgmt report)
- **5G network launched in Austria (January 2020)** after acquiring spectrum (incl. 2,100 MHz), covering ~25% of the population, with acceleration planned for 2021. (mgmt report)
- Formalised **ESG strategy with science-based climate targets** (energy per TB; Paris-aligned). (mgmt report)
- **Net result €388.8 mn**; free cash flow rose to €503.7 mn. (DB)

## Notes
- 5G + fibre become the dual investment narrative from here. Net debt now reported on incl-/excl-leases bases (IFRS 16); the DB uses the excl-leases series for continuity.
