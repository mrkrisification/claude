# FY2018 — Group-wide rebranding

*Source: `2018_TAG_Annual_Financial_Report2018.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2018`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Group-wide rebranding** toward the unified A1 identity; rebranding/advertising costs (and brand amortisation) weighed on results — a ~€180.8 mn financial-result item is noted in connection with the rebranding and a Slovenia-related 45% stake transaction. (p~, mgmt report)
- Continued fibre/RGU demand growth; Bulgaria +2.7%. (mgmt report)
- Net result €242.7 mn. (DB)

## Notes
- Last year on the **pre-IFRS 16** basis. The FY2018 EBITDA is restated upward (1,380.6 → 1,548.9) in the FY2019 report when IFRS 16 takes effect — the key restatement in the database (see `metrics/ebitda.md`).
