# FY2004 — Free float opens up; the MobilTel (Bulgaria) option

*Source: `2004_Annual-Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2004`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Telecom Italia completed its withdrawal (Jan 2004)**; in December **ÖIAG cut its stake by 85 mn shares, lifting the free float to 69.8%**. (p7)
- **Swisscom merger talks** surfaced and subsided; management open under conditions, but committed to standalone growth. Share price **+40%**, well above the sector. (p7)
- **Call-option agreement to acquire 100% of MobilTel**, the leading mobile operator in high-growth Bulgaria — the springboard for the CEE expansion. (p7)
- Results surpassed the company's own targets. (p7)

## Notes
- Sets up the 2005 MobilTel close; ownership shifts from a Telecom-Italia-anchored register toward a broad free float + the state holding (ÖIAG).
