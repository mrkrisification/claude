# FY2007 — Belarus entry; debt-funded expansion lifts leverage

*Source: `2007_TAG_Annual-Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2007`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Market entry in Belarus** (acquisition of MDC / velcom), alongside the build-out in **Serbia and the Republic of Macedonia**. (p3)
- **Revenue +3.3% to €4,919.0 mn**; strong CEE mobile growth (Bulgaria 5.1 mn customers, Belarus 3.1 mn). (p3)
- But **EBITDA −3% to €1,854.9 mn** and **net income −12.3% to €492.5 mn**, on higher interest costs. (p9)
- **Net debt jumped ~39% to €4,407 mn** — debt-funded M&A plus share buybacks. (p9)
- Dividend €0.75/share. (p9)

## Notes
- The acquisition-led growth strategy peaks here on leverage; the velcom (Belarus) business becomes a long-running, sometimes geopolitically sensitive, group asset.
