# FY2016 — Convergence via bolt-on M&A across CEE

*Source: `2016_TAG_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2016`).*

## Highlights & events
- Now consolidated as **"Part of América Móvil"**. (mgmt report)
- **velcom (Belarus) became a convergent operator** via the **acquisition of Atlant Telecom and its subsidiary TeleSet**; further convergence M&A in Croatia. (mgmt report)
- **Revenue +2.1%** — M&A-driven, despite retail-roaming revenue losses; launched the A1 hybrid (fixed + mobile) modem. (mgmt report, DB)
- Net result €413.2 mn. (DB)
