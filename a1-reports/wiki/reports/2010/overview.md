# FY2010 — Austrian fixed+mobile merge into "A1"

*Source: `2010_TAG_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2010`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Merged the Austrian fixed-line and mobile operations into a single new company, A1 Telekom Austria** — the convergence step that follows the 2009 board approval. (p1, p5)
- Cover timeline recaps the group's build: 2000 IPO → Slovenia (Si.mobil) → Bulgaria (Mobiltel) → Serbia/Belarus (velcom) → Macedonia → the A1 merger. (p5)
- Convergence and data-volume growth across CEE; Vip mobile (Serbia) +17.8% to ~1.4 mn; Si.mobil rolling out HSPA+. (p5+)
- Revenue €4,650.8 mn (−3.1%); headline EBITDA measure relabelled to **"EBITDA comparable"** (€1,645.9 mn). (DB)

## Notes
- Start of the "A1" single-brand identity in Austria; the EBITDA-label change matters for cross-year comparison (see `metrics/ebitda.md`).
