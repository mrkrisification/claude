# FY2009 — Austrian fixed+mobile merger; convergence push

*Source: `2009_Annual_report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2009`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Supervisory Board approved merging the domestic Fixed Net and Mobile Communication operations in Austria** — the structural basis for convergence. (p3)
- Group served **~18.9 mn mobile customers across 8 markets**: Austria, Bulgaria, Belarus, Croatia, Slovenia, Liechtenstein, Serbia and the Republic of Macedonia. (p3)
- **Convergent bundles** (fixed + mobile + broadband + TV) pushed; broadband lines +15.5% to >1.0 mn. (p3)
- Revenue ~€4.8 bn; **net income recovered to €94.9 mn** after the 2008 restructuring year. (DB)
- Dividend policy formalised: **65% of net income, floor €0.75/share**. (p3)

## Notes
- "velcom"/Belarus and the SEE cluster are now core. The Austrian convergence merger foreshadows the single-brand "A1" identity adopted in the following years.
