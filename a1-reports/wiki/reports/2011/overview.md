# FY2011 — Group-wide "A1" brand; Belarus impairment drives a loss

*Source: `2011_TAG_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2011`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Introduction of the single brand "A1"** beyond Austria as the group's unifying identity. (mgmt report)
- **Acquisition of B.net in Croatia (Aug 2011)** — cable, pay-TV and internet, strengthening Vipnet's convergence. (mgmt report)
- **Net loss of €252.8 mn**, driven by a **€279.0 mn impairment of the Belarusian subsidiary velcom** plus **€233.7 mn of restructuring charges**; cumulative restructuring provisions reached €888.8 mn by year-end. (mgmt report)
- Regulatory headwinds: roaming and mobile-termination-rate cuts. (mgmt report)

## Notes
- First clear instance of CEE goodwill impairment (velcom) hitting the group result — a recurring theme as currency/regulatory shocks hit individual markets.
