# FY2014 — América Móvil becomes majority owner; capital increase

*Source: `TA_Annual_Report_2014_en.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2014`).*

## Highlights & events
- **América Móvil became the majority shareholder** — the control change after its 2012 entry. (p18)
- **Successful capital increase (rights issue)** to strengthen the balance sheet after the spectrum spend. (p18)
- **Acquired blizoo**, the leading Macedonian cable operator, and **merged Vip and One in Macedonia into "one.Vip"**. (mgmt report)
- Impairment in Bulgaria contributed to a **net loss of €185.4 mn**; ultra-high-bandwidth/convergence push. (DB, mgmt report)

## Notes
- Ownership now: América Móvil (majority) + ÖIAG (state) under a syndicate agreement. This pairing frames governance for the next decade.
