# FY2022 — "A1 Group" rebrand; growth despite tough macro

*Source: `2022_A1_Group_Report_2022.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2022`).*

## Highlights & events
- Group now presents as **"A1 Group"** (rebranded from A1 Telekom Austria Group). (report title / mgmt report)
- Delivered **both revenue and EBITDA growth** through a difficult macro (high inflation, CEE FX volatility). (p5)
- Continued **5G monetisation**: A1 Austria launched a new mobile portfolio (Nov 2022), moving 5G from premium-only into nearly all tariffs; similar 5G-in-all-tariffs moves in Croatia and Slovenia. (p~)
- **Net result jumped to €635 mn**; EBIT €871 mn. (DB)

## Notes
- The "A1 Group" identity is the current brand. Strong 2022 profit sets up the tower spin-off the following year.
