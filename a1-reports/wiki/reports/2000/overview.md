# FY2000 — IPO year: transformation, Jet2Web, NYSE, operating loss

*Source: `2000_Annual-Report.pdf` (first year reported under **US GAAP**). Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2000`).*

## Highlights & events
- **IPO on 21 November 2000** — the biggest capital-market transaction in Austria to date and the first Austrian company listed on the **NYSE**. Free float 22.4% (ÖIAG 47.8%, Telecom Italia 29.8%); ~90,000 shareholders. (p13, p10)
- **New management board** (CEO Heinz Sundt) appointed April 2000; comprehensive reorganisation, incl. the "W.E.R.T." restructuring programme. (p6, p9)
- **New umbrella brand "Jet2Web"** launched June 2000, bundling fixed/internet/multimedia. (p6, p11)
- **Acquired Czech On Line** (Czech ISP market leader); launched first nationwide **GPRS** network and first WAP portal. (p6)
- Switched financial reporting to **US GAAP** for the new international shareholder base. (p10)
- **EBITDA fell 31% to €1,016.5 mn; operating loss €−31.5 mn** — restructuring plus the global telecom-sector downturn. (p10)

## Notes
- The defining structural year: state monopoly → listed company. Heavy UMTS-era investment and restructuring depressed reported profitability despite revenue growth.
