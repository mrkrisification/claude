# FY1999 — Competition bites, mobile booms, IPO groundwork

*Source: `1999_Geschäftsbericht.pdf` (figures presented in both EUR and ATS). Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=1999`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Competition intensified sharply**: the number of alternative fixed-voice providers quadrupled and fixed voice-telephony market share fell to ~85%. (p13)
- **Mobile penetration in Austria doubled**; Mobilkom recorded the most net adds and launched the first Austrian **dual-band (GSM 1800 MHz)** network. (p8, p13)
- **Partnership with Telecom Italia**; cooperation with the LIBRO retail chain for internet/terminal-equipment sales. (p13)
- Croatian mobile operator **VIPnet consolidated** into the group from 1999. (p3)
- **EBITDA margin collapsed 52.8% → 39.6%** as competition eroded fixed-line profitability; net income €0.23 bn. (p3)
- After year-end (March 2000) the supervisory board set the course for the **planned IPO**. (p13)

## Notes
- Last full year before the November 2000 IPO; the margin drop here is the start of the long competitive normalisation, not a one-off.
