# FY2024 — First full year after the tower spin-off

*Source: `2024_A1_AnnualFinancialReport_EN.pdf` (English). Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2024`).*

## Highlights & events
- First full year reflecting the **EuroTeleSites tower spin-off**: depreciation rose sharply (tower lease-backs), so **EBIT declined to €861 mn despite EBITDA growth to €2,021 mn**. (mgmt report, DB)
- **Value-protection (price indexation) measures** in Austria, Bulgaria and Croatia to offset prior-years' inflation; CEE grew while **Austria was in recession**. (mgmt report)
- **Austria segment = 52% of group revenue**; record revenue €5,413 mn. (mgmt report, DB)
- **Net result €627 mn**; net-debt definition changed in Q4-2024 (now includes short-term investments). (DB)

## Notes
- The EBIT/EBITDA divergence from FY2024 is structural (tower spin-off), not operational. The original FY2024 report was German (`Ergebnisbericht`); the English version was substituted.
