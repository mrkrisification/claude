# FY2015 — Recovery: +6.7% EBITDA, biggest-ever infrastructure programme

*Source: `2015_TAG_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2015`).*

## Highlights & events
- **EBITDA comparable +6.7%**; net result recovered to €392.8 mn after the 2014 loss. (p8, DB)
- Launched the **largest infrastructure programme in the company's history** (fibre + LTE); **LTE live in five countries**, incl. rollout in Serbia. (p8)
- **Acquisition of Amis** (Slovenia/Serbia); **one.Vip** established in Macedonia. (p8)
- Multi-brand strategy (no-frills "bob", "YESSS!"); "A1 Guru" advisory service introduced. (p8)

## Notes
- Marks the turn from impairment-driven losses to steady, infrastructure-led growth that continues into the A1-branded era.
