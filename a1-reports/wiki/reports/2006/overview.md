# FY2006 — Scaling the CEE footprint (~13 mn customers)

*Source: `2006_TAG_Annual_Report-.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2006`).*

## Highlights & events
- Group serving **~13 mn customers**, with subsidiaries now spanning **Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, Serbia and the Republic of Macedonia**. (p1, p3)
- **Revenue +9.0% to €4,759.6 mn**; Adjusted EBITDA €1,906.8 mn (the period's peak); earnings per share rose. (DB, p3)
- Telekom Austria among the most heavily traded stocks on the Vienna exchange. (p1)

## Notes
- The wireless segment's CEE growth now clearly outweighs the structurally declining Austrian fixed-line voice business. Serbia and Macedonia appear as new mobile markets.
