# FY2023 — Spin-off of the tower business (EuroTeleSites)

*Source: `2023_A1-Group-Financial-Report_full.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2023`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Spin-off of "EuroTeleSites AG"** — the group's tower/passive-infrastructure business carved out and separately listed. (p6)
- Accelerated **fibre** (Austria network ~73,000 km) and **5G to ~85% of the Austrian population**; continued 5G in the international footprint. (mgmt report)
- **A1 Croatia acquired 15-year spectrum for €111 mn**; Bulgaria acquired 700 MHz spectrum. (mgmt report)
- **Net result €646 mn**; capex elevated at €1,093 mn, so **free cash flow fell to €354 mn**. (DB)

## Notes
- EuroTeleSites is the key structural event: from FY2024 the tower lease-backs raise depreciation and depress EBIT, so post-2023 EBIT/EBITDA comparisons need this caveat.
