# FY2005 — MobilTel (Bulgaria) closes: the big CEE leap

*Source: `2005_Annual-Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2005`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Completed the acquisition of MobilTel (Bulgaria)** — at the time the **largest acquisition ever by an Austrian company** — and integrated it rapidly. (p5, p7)
- **€1,000 mn bond placed** early in 2005 to fund the purchase. (p7)
- Mobile segment reached **~9 mn customers** across Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, Slovenia and Liechtenstein. (p5)
- **Dividend more than doubled.** (p7)
- Revenue rose ~7.9% to €4,377.3 mn; Adjusted EBITDA €1,757.2 mn. (DB)

## Notes
- The structural inflection point: from an Austria-centric incumbent to a CEE mobile-led group. Mobile scale now drives revenue and EBITDA growth.
