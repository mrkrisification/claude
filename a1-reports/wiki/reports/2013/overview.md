# FY2013 — €1.03 bn spectrum auction, capex spike, rating cut

*Source: `TA_Annual_Report_2013_en.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2013`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Austrian multiband spectrum auction**: A1 acquired **2×70 MHz — 50% of the spectrum on offer, including two-thirds of the 800 MHz band — for €1.03 bn**. (mgmt report)
- That auction drove **capex to a spike of €1,779 mn** (vs ~€730 mn typical). (DB)
- The associated borrowing led to **rating downgrades to BBB-/Baa2 (S&P / Moody's)**; group targeted a return to BBB via operational deleveraging. (mgmt report)
- Ongoing Austrian restructuring (social-plan offers to fixed-line staff). Net result €109.7 mn. (mgmt report, DB)

## Notes
- The 2013 capex outlier in any chart is this auction, not an operational shift. Leverage and ratings become a managed theme from here.
