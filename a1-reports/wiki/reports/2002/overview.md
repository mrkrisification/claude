# FY2002 — Back to profit; mobilkom bought back; Wireline/Wireless reorg

*Source: `2002_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2002`).*

## Highlights & events
- Management called it **"the most successful year since liberalisation."** (p6)
- **Repurchased the mobilkom Austria minority stake from Telecom Italia Mobile (June 2002)** — Telekom Austria again sole owner of its mobile arm. (p6)
- **Reorganised into two reporting segments — Wireline and Wireless** (effective from 2003), completing the shift from a product/infrastructure company to a full-service provider. (p6)
- **Returned to net profit (€12.8 mn)** after two consecutive loss years; total managed revenue ~€3.9 bn. (DB, p1)
- Strengthened domestic market leadership across all segments. (p6)

## Notes
- Consolidating ownership of mobilkom set up the group's later CEE mobile-led growth. The Wireline/Wireless structure persists (in evolved form) for years.
