# FY2019 — IFRS 16 takes effect; 5G-ready spectrum

*Source: `2019_TAG_Annual_Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2019`).*

## Highlights & events
- **IFRS 16 (lease accounting) adopted** — capitalising leases lifts headline EBITDA and introduces **"EBITDA after leases" (EBITDAaL)** as the comparable measure. (mgmt report; see `metrics/ebitda.md`)
- **Acquired frequencies in Austria (incl. 3.5 GHz) and Belarus** to prepare for 5G; the headline operating measure is relabelled **"EBIT"**. (mgmt report)
- Strong **fibre connection growth (+30.8%)**; price adjustments supported results. (mgmt report)
- **Net result €327.4 mn (+34.4%).** (DB)

## Notes
- The single most important definitional break in the dataset: from FY2019 EBITDA is on the IFRS 16 basis, and EBITDAaL is the line comparable to the old pre-IFRS16 EBITDA.
