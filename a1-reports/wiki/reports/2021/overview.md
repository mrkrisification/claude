# FY2021 — 5G + fibre build-out, rising dividend

*Source: `2021_A1_TAG_Combined-Annual-Report_eng.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2021`).*

## Highlights & events
- Accelerated **5G roll-out** (62% of the Austrian population at fastest mobile) and continued investment in **Austria's biggest fibre network**. (mgmt report)
- **Spectrum acquired in Croatia** in the 700 MHz, 3.6 GHz and 26 GHz bands. (mgmt report)
- **Dividend raised to €0.28/share** proposed, reflecting earnings growth; "Empowering digital life" vision and sustainability targets. (mgmt report)
- **Net result €455.0 mn.** (DB)

## Notes
- Last full year under the "A1 Telekom Austria Group" name before the 2022 "A1 Group" rebrand.
