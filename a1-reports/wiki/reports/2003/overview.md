# FY2003 — First dividend, UMTS launch, Telecom Italia exits

*Source: `2003_Annual-Report.pdf`. Numbers in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=2003`).*

## Highlights & events
- **First-ever dividend payment**, reflecting improved earnings. (p10)
- **Telecom Italia's full withdrawal** announced/executed (completed Jan 2004), lifting free float to ~52.8%. (p10, p18)
- **Commercial launch of Europe's first national UMTS network (April 2003)**; first UMTS call in Croatia (May 2003); one of Europe's first EDGE networks in Slovenia (Dec 2003). (p33)
- Active **evaluation of a Bulgarian expansion (MobilTel)** — not yet concluded this year. (p7)
- Vodafone partnership (roaming, Si.mobil). (p7); new Austrian Telecommunications Act 2003 redefined the market into 18 sub-markets. (p37)
- First full-year consolidation of mobilkom (bought back June 2002); **Adjusted EBITDA +3.1% to €1,509.8 mn**. (p38)

## Notes
- Reported under US GAAP; "Adjusted EBITDA" is the headline EBITDA measure here (see `metrics/ebitda.md`).
