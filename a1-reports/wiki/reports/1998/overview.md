# FY1998 — First year of full market liberalisation

*Source: `1998_Finanzbericht.pdf`, `1998_Geschäftsbericht.pdf`. Figures originally in **Austrian Schilling (ATS)**; EUR-million values in the DB come from the FY2000 report's 3-year summary. Numbers live in `data/financials.duckdb` (`fiscal_year=1998`).*

## Highlights & events
- **Full liberalisation of the Austrian telecom market** from 1 Jan 1998 under the Telecommunications Act (TKG); asymmetrical regulation and the Telekom-Control-Kommission's interconnection rulings shaped the year. (1998 Finanzbericht, p5)
- Telekom Austria remained the **dominant fixed-network incumbent** — ~50% fixed penetration, ~92% digital exchanges. (p7)
- **Mobilkom Austria's "A1" GSM brand passed 1.28 mn digital subscribers (+86% YoY)**, 97% coverage via 2,657 base stations. (p7)
- Group shaped by the **spin-off from the federal postal administration (PTA)**, which left a heavy debt/negative-interest burden. (p7)
- Efficiency drive toward international benchmarks **in preparation for a planned IPO**. (p5)
- Turnover ATS 46.6 bn; operating margin 24.6%; EBITDA(D) ATS 24,642 mn. (p33–34)

## Notes
- The two 1998 PDFs are the earliest in the archive and report in ATS. The high reported 1998 EBITDA margin (~52.8%, per the FY1999 report) reflects the pre-competition monopoly cost base.
