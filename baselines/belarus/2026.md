## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
Belarus's mobile market entered 2026 defined by a single structural event: the April 2025 presidential decree (No. 139) mandating a state-controlled, single-infrastructure 5G rollout under beCloud, foreclosing independent operator network builds. Competitive intensity on infrastructure is effectively neutralised — operators compete on tariffs and service overlay, not network ownership. MTS holds the subscriber lead (~47% share), A1 is second (~39%), and life:) trails distantly (~12%), a gap that has not materially shifted. The market's 11.4 million connections (127% penetration) leave minimal room for net subscriber growth, pushing competition toward ARPU defence.

### Last 90 days

- **Infrastructure** — Lukashenko signed Decree No. 139 on 1 April 2025, designating beCloud as sole 5G infrastructure operator for a decade-long rollout; beCloud granted tax/regulatory preferences for 10 years, locking in the shared-network model. ([president.gov.by](https://president.gov.by/en/events/podpisan-ukaz-o-realizacii-investicionnogo-proekta-1743610954), 2025-04-01; [eng.belta.by](https://eng.belta.by/president/view/lukashenko-signs-decree-on-investment-project-to-introduce-5g-technology-in-belarus-166792-2025/), 2025-04-01) — Removes infrastructure as a competitive lever; operators are reduced to service resellers on 5G.

- **5G launch — life:)** — life:) became the first operator to fully commercialise 5G in Belarus, with live service in Minsk, Brest, Vitsyebsk, Mahiliou and Homiel; coverage supported by >1,000 beCloud 5G base stations. ([reform.news](https://reform.news/en/life-launches-5g-network), 2025-2026) — Allows life:) to claim a "first-to-5G" narrative despite its weak subscriber position; a rare marketing win for the #3 player.

- **5G launch — MTS** — MTS announced and launched 5G service in Belarus, operating across 1,100 active 5G base stations via beCloud. ([reform.news](https://reform.news/en/belarusian-mts-announces-launch-of-5g-service), 2026; [foro3d.com](https://foro3d.com/en/2026/abril/mts-lanza-red-5g-completa-pero-en-bielorrusia-y-no-en-rusia.html), 2026-04) — MTS's Belarus 5G commercialisation is notable given its Russian parent has yet to launch 5G at home; Belarus becomes a showcase market.

- **Tariff / pricing** — MTS launched new "Comfortable" tariff tier in February 2025 with free incoming calls (15 min/day) and revised roaming terms. ([mordorintelligence.com](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/belarus-telecom-market), 2025) — Incremental differentiation; roaming revision may target frequent cross-border travellers, a segment under pressure from sanctions-era travel patterns.

- **Regulatory pricing** — An April 2025 decree introduced mandatory basic social tariffs, capping operator pricing flexibility on core plans. ([mordorintelligence.com](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/belarus-telecom-market), 2025) — Compresses ARPU upside; limits revenue-per-user growth even as data consumption rises.

- **Network sharing — A1** — A1 announced RAN-sharing projects in December 2024 to improve efficiency ahead of 5G transition. ([mordorintelligence.com](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/belarus-telecom-market), 2024-12) — Signals A1 is rationalising cost base rather than investing aggressively; defensive posture consistent with an operator in the #2 position with limited share-gain prospects.

**Directional verdict:** Stable — Competitive intensity is structurally contained by state-mandated shared 5G infrastructure and social tariff caps; operators are differentiated by service overlay and brand rather than network or price, and no market share disruption is imminent.

### Outlook to year-end

- **5G Phase 1 completion (Minsk, 2025–2027 target):** beCloud aims for 70% Minsk territorial coverage within two years of the April 2025 decree. Completion of Minsk coverage would push operators to compete on 5G service quality/bundles — marginally intensifying retail competition if reached on schedule; delay is the more likely outcome.

- **Social tariff reviews:** Regulatory pricing mandates are subject to periodic government review. Any upward revision to permitted tariff floors would allow ARPU recovery and ease competitive pressure; a freeze or tightening would further suppress differentiation.

- **beCloud 4G/5G integration rollout to regional cities:** Phase 2 coverage (regional centres beyond the five currently live cities) expected 2026–2027. life:)'s first-mover position in regional 5G is only an advantage while MTS and A1 catch up — the window is short.
