# Croatia Telecom Market — Structural Baseline

**Last updated:** 2026-06-09
**Covers:** 2024–2026 (structural history)

---

## Market Structure

Three-player oligopoly. Tele2 Croatia ceased to exist as a brand in 2020 (rebranded Telemach after United Group acquisition).

| Operator | Owner | Mobile share (subs) | Revenue trend | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hrvatski Telekom (HT) | Deutsche Telekom (51%+) | ~46% (⚠ see note) | FY2025 rev €1,142M, EBITDA AL €430M, net €143M (HT Group, incl. Montenegro) | Market leader; fixed dominant; FTTP build-out leader |
| Telemach Croatia | United Group | ~35% (⚠ see note) | Private; standalone accounts public via FINA — FY2025 rev €328M, EBITDA €107M, net €8.6M | Formerly Tele2; 98.9% 5G pop. coverage (end-2024); aggressive pricing |
| A1 Croatia | A1 Group (Telekom Austria) | ~20% (⚠ see note) | FY2024 segment rev €570M, EBITDA €224M (+18.7%) | ~2.16M mobile subs; convergent cable+mobile; EBITDA margin ~39% |

> ⚠ **Mobile-share figures (46/35/20) are unreconciled and likely stale/mislabeled.** They contradict the
> operators' own reported bases (A1 alone reports ~2.16M mobile subs — not a 20% share). HAKOM publishes
> whole-market totals only (no operator split), and operator "subscribers" vs HAKOM "mobile lines" use
> different definitions. See `company-research/_markets/croatia/market-overview.md` for the reconciliation.

**Total telecom revenues:** EUR 1.87B (2025), +3.6% YoY (HAKOM). Fixed +5.1%, mobile +2.9%.
**MNO market value:** ~USD 1.91B (2025), projected USD 2.18B by 2030 (CAGR 2.58%).
**Mobile broadband subscribers:** ~5.69M (2025).

## Competitive History (2024–2026)

**2024: Coverage parity and EBITDA recovery**
- All three operators on 5G since 2021; coverage parity reached in urban areas; differentiation shifted to OTT bundling, FMC convergence, and enterprise 5G/IoT.
- A1 Croatia EBITDA +18.7% FY2024 — strongest single-operator growth in the period; driven by convergent cable+mobile offer and cost discipline.
- Telemach claimed 98.9% 5G population coverage by end-2024, competing directly with HT's network quality lead.
- HAKOM removed SMP designation for HT in 72 local markets (where alternative operator VHCN covers ≥33% of users AND HT retail share <50%) — material deregulation step reducing HT's wholesale pricing obligations in competitive zones.

**2025: Synchronized price hikes and HT 3G shutdown**
- March 2025: HT and A1 raised tariffs ~3% (inflation-indexed). Telemach made no public matching announcement.
- April 2025: HT completed Croatia's first national 3G network shutdown, refarming 900 MHz and 2100 MHz to 4G/5G capacity.
- HT FY2025: Revenue +3.6%, EBITDA AL +3.3%, net profit €143M (+0.8%), capex €268.4M (+10.5% — peak investment year).
- A1 Croatia Q2 2025: Revenue +11% YoY; Q1 2025: EBITDA +22.8% — consistently outperforming peers on financial metrics.
- Total Croatian telecom revenues reached EUR 1.87B in 2025 (HAKOM).

**2026: Second synchronized price hike and fiber acceleration**
- March 2026: HT and A1 raised prices +3.7% (matching Croatia's official 2025 average inflation); billed from April 2026.
- HT Q1 2026: Net profit +12.3% YoY — acceleration after FY2025 capex peak.
- HT completing €243M fiber investment in Dubrovnik-Neretva County; EU/NRRP rural fiber rollout with June 2026 completion targets for sub-projects.
- HT deploying private 5G at Zagreb and Pula airports (drone/camera use cases).
- A1 Croatia 3G shutdown expected by end-2026.
- No M&A activity in the period.

## Infrastructure State

**5G:**
- All three operators licensed and live since 2021; spectrum: 700 MHz (n28) + 3.5 GHz (n78); no new auction since 2021 (€48M raised)
- Telemach: 98.9% 5G population coverage (end-2024) — most aggressive rollout
- HT: Coverage leader on quality metrics (888.2 network score); 3G off April 2025
- A1: 3G off by end-2026; 5G coverage competitive but not quantified publicly
- EOLO (Italy): 4th licence holder; no public rollout data

**Fixed/FTTH:**
- HT: Dominant fixed incumbent; FTTP coverage 62.1% of households (2023), targeting ~75% (~1.1M/1.5M households) by 2025–2026; €243M Dubrovnik investment; EU/NRRP rural build funding secured; mmWave FWA being piloted
- A1 Croatia: Convergent cable+mobile; exact fiber coverage not publicly itemised
- Telemach: €100M fiber build committed as part of €230M total investment plan

## Regulatory History

**Key regulator:** HAKOM (Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Industries); competition authority: AZTN.

**2021:** 5G spectrum auction (€48M); 700 MHz, 3.6 GHz, 26 GHz awarded to HT, A1, Telemach, EOLO.

**2024:** HAKOM removed HT's SMP designation in 72 competitive local markets. HAKOM designated Croatia's DSA national coordinator.

**2025:** No new spectrum auctions. HAKOM Annual Report 2024 adopted by Parliament (October 2025). HT 3G shutdown completed (April 2025).

**2026:** No new regulatory interventions identified. SMP deregulation in 72 markets remains in effect.

## Key Tensions to Track

1. **Telemach financial resilience** — Private (United Group parent); no investor-relations disclosure, but standalone accounts are public via FINA (FY2025 rev €328M, EBITDA €107M, thin net €8.6M → margin/leverage worth watching). If parent-group pressure materialises, the €230M capex commitment could be deferred, benefiting HT
2. **HT capex normalization** — FY2025 was peak capex; 2026 guidance is "moderate capex decline"; watch whether fiber build pace slows or accelerates on NRRP funding
3. **A1 growth sustainability** — Exceptional EBITDA growth 2024–2025; sustainability depends on convergence bundle take-up in 2026
4. **HAKOM SMP deregulation scope** — Expansion beyond 72 markets would further erode HT's fixed dominance
5. **Synchronized pricing pattern** — HT and A1 moving in lockstep on CPI-indexed hikes; if one defects with promotional cuts, triggers a price war
6. **EOLO rollout** — Fourth 5G licence holder has not publicly launched; activation as a price disruptor in regional markets would spike competitive intensity
