# Croatia Telecom — 2026 Annual Log

## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
The Croatian market entered 2026 with the two dominant operators (HT and A1) executing simultaneous CPI-linked price increases — a coordinated pricing signal that dampens aggressive discounting. HT has sustained revenue momentum (Q1 2026 revenue +1.6% YoY, net profit +12.3%) while Telemach, now fully rebranded from Tele2 under United Group, competes on 5G coverage and bundle pricing rather than headline rate cuts. The structural dynamic is consolidation around three operators and a shift from subscriber volume wars to ARPU extraction.

### Last 90 days

- **Price increase** — HT and A1 Croatia raised fixed and mobile tariff prices by 3.7% effective 1 March 2026, in line with Croatia's 2025 average inflation rate; customers saw new prices on April bills. This is a coordinated margin-defense move reducing the risk of a price war in H1. ([Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/hrvatski-telekom-and-a1-croatia-to-raise-prices-from-1-march--1560323), 2026)

- **HT Q1 2026 earnings** — HT Group revenue reached EUR 271.9 million (+1.6% YoY); mobile service revenue EUR 100.3 million; Adjusted EBITDA AL EUR 98.4 million (+2.5%), margin 36.2%; net profit EUR 29.9 million (+12.3%). Solid profitability suggests HT is not sacrificing margin to defend share. ([SeeNews / Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/hrvatski-telekom-group-sees-revenue-and-profit-growth-in-q1--1569906), 2026)

- **5G infrastructure — airports** — HT deployed private 5G networks at Croatian airports (Zagreb, Pula, Zadar) enabling AI-assisted inspections and drone trials from 2026, reinforcing its enterprise 5G narrative over consumer rivals. ([TeckNexus](https://tecknexus.com/5gusecase/hrvatski-telekoms-private-5g-networks-transform-croatias-airports/), 2026)

- **Rural fiber subsidy** — A EUR 101.4 million EU Recovery and Resilience Facility-backed open-access fiber scheme is progressing through network infrastructure installation, targeting underserved counties at 100 Mbps symmetrical. This expands the addressable fixed-broadband base and will intensify fixed competition when live. ([EC Digital Connectivity Croatia](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-connectivity-croatia), 2025-2026)

- **3G switch-off** — Croatia completed its 3G shutdown in January 2025; freed spectrum has been refarmed to accelerate 4G capacity and 5G rollout. Telemach claims 98.9% population coverage on 5G by end-2024, putting pressure on HT's network leadership claim. ([Mordor Intelligence / BuddeComm](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/croatia-telecom-mno-market), 2025)

- **Market saturation** — Active SIM density exceeded 119% in 2024 against a 5.69 million subscriber base; operators have pivoted to ARPU-growth strategies via premium data bundles, convergent FMC offers, and device leasing — limiting the scope for subscriber-poaching campaigns. ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/croatia-telecom-mno-market), 2025)

**Directional verdict:** Stable — Coordinated price hikes by HT and A1 act as a tacit margin floor; Telemach competes on coverage and bundles rather than price, keeping intensity contained despite a three-player structure.

### Outlook to year-end

- **EU fiber scheme activation** — Rural open-access fiber (EUR 101.4M) expected to reach service availability in late 2026/early 2027; if it lands on schedule it will extend fixed-broadband competition into markets currently served only by HT's copper, benefiting Telemach and A1 as wholesale access users.

- **United Group / Telemach strategic review** — United Group has divested non-EU assets (Serbian operations sold February 2025) and is consolidating around EU markets; any further M&A moves involving Telemach Croatia could reshape the three-player dynamic — timing uncertain but watch for H2 2026 announcements.

- **HAKOM spectrum consultation** — HAKOM opened a new spectrum consultation (reference: Developing Telecoms, 2025); if a follow-on allocation or mid-band refarming is decided in H2 2026, it could shift 5G competitive positioning, particularly for A1 which holds the weakest 5G coverage narrative currently.
