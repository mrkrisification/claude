# Macedonia Telecom Market — 2026 Log

## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
North Macedonia's mobile market has been defined in the first half of 2026 by a single structural event: the entry of One Macedonia (backed by Hungarian group 4iG) as the country's third mobile network operator, following its spectrum award in January 2026. Makedonski Telekom has maintained strong incumbent momentum — posting 6% revenue growth and subscriber gains across all segments in Q1 2026 — while A1 Macedonia faces the dual pressure of a well-resourced new entrant building out a greenfield 5G network and a dominant rival still taking share. Lycamobile continues to operate only as an MVNO with no licensed spectrum.

### Last 90 days
- **New entrant activation** — One Macedonia (4iG) was selected as the sole qualifying bidder in the AEC 5G tender, paid EUR 8.02 million in spectrum fees on 14 January 2026, and was formally licensed as the third MNO. This introduces a structurally new competitive axis — a 5G-only greenfield challenger — for the first time in the market's history. ([MIA / AEC, 2026-01-12](https://mia.mk/en/story/one-macedonia-granted-license-to-be-countrys-third-mobile-operator))
- **One Macedonia × Ericsson network deal** — One Macedonia signed a general contract with Ericsson to develop its 5G network, signalling serious build commitment and fast time-to-service intent. Pressure on incumbents to accelerate their own 5G roadmaps will increase. ([Albanian Daily News, 2026](https://albaniandailynews.com/news/one-macedonia-and-ericsson-sign-general-contract-on-developing-state-of-the-art-5g-network))
- **Makedonski Telekom Q1 2026 revenue +6% YoY** — Operating revenues reached MKD 2.96 billion; mobile subscribers grew 1.9% to 1.28 million (48.7% postpaid); fixed broadband +3.5% to 226,454; TV +6.1% to 171,428. Net profit +1% to MKD 542.4 million. Incumbency dividend is still compounding but growth rates are modest. ([Telecompaper, 2026](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/makedonski-telekom-reports-6-revenue-growth-in-q1--1569960))
- **Customer growth across all segments** — Separate Telecompaper report confirmed Makedonski Telekom gained customers in mobile, fixed voice, broadband, and TV in Q1 2026 — suggesting A1 is under pressure across the full quad-play stack, not just mobile. ([Telecompaper, 2026](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/makedonski-telekom-sees-customer-growth-across-all-segments-in-q1--1570945))
- **Spectrum bands allocated to new entrant: 700 MHz / 1800 MHz / 3 GHz** — Coverage obligation of one city by end-March 2027, main corridors by end-2028, all cities by 2030, national 100 Mbps minimum by 2032. The 700 MHz anchor band gives One Macedonia meaningful indoor and rural reach from day one. ([C-PRAV / AEC, 2025-04-24](https://c-prav.com/2025/04/24/north-macedonia-launches-5g-spectrum-tender-for-new-market-entrants/))
- **Lycamobile status** — Lycamobile operates as an MVNO only; it holds no licensed spectrum and is not a structural competitive factor at the network layer. ([IMEI.info carrier check, 2026](https://www.imei.info/carriers/north-macedonia/cosmofon/))

**Directional verdict:** Intensifying — The entry of One Macedonia as a licensed 5G MNO is the most significant structural disruption in the Macedonian market in over a decade; competitive pressure will rise materially as One begins commercial service, forcing both incumbents to respond on pricing and network investment.

### Outlook to year-end
- **One Macedonia first-city 5G launch (deadline: end-March 2027)** — Commercial launch could be pulled forward to late 2026 given the Ericsson contract signing; if so, it will immediately trigger a pricing and bundling response from Makedonski Telekom and A1, pushing ARPU compression across the market.
- **A1 Macedonia strategic response** — A1 Group has yet to signal any pricing or network investment counter-move; a failure to respond before One Macedonia's launch would accelerate subscriber losses. Watch for A1 promotional campaigns or a network upgrade announcement in H2 2026.
- **Makedonski Telekom full-year 2026 results (expected Q1 2027)** — If the 6% revenue growth trajectory holds through H2, it will confirm that incumbent market power is durable even as One Macedonia enters; a deceleration would signal earlier-than-expected competitive impact.
