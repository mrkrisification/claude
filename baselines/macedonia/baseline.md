# North Macedonia Telecom Market — Structural Baseline

**Last updated:** 2026-06-09
**Covers:** 2024–2026 (structural history)

---

## Market Structure

Near-duopoly transitioning to three-player market. Lycamobile (the legacy third operator) was acquired by Mtel Bosnia; One Macedonia (4iG Group) was awarded the new third mobile licence in early 2026.

| Operator | Owner | Mobile share (end-2025) | Revenue | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Makedonija | A1 Group (Telekom Austria) | **47.01%** | ~USD 201.7M (2025) | Slight subscriber leader; eSIM on postpaid; rebranded from One in recent years |
| Makedonski Telekom | Deutsche Telekom / Magyar Telekom | **46.37%** | ~USD 188.4M (2025) | Fixed incumbent; 5G since Feb 2022; capex €40M/year; TV+broadband convergence |
| One Macedonia | 4iG Group (Hungary) | New entrant — 0% at end-2025 | — | Won 5G spectrum (Jan 2026); Ericsson 5G SA deal; commercial launch 2026; coverage obligations by 2028–2030 |
| Lycamobile | Now owned by Mtel (Bosnia) | ~6.6% (pre-acquisition) | — | Acquired by Mtel; future commercial strategy unclear |

**Note on revenue data:** ZoomInfo aggregated figures are indicative only; Makedonski Telekom is the only operator with audited public financials (listed on Macedonian Stock Exchange).

**Total mobile lines:** 1.99M active (end-2025), +3.8% YoY; 2.65M total connections (146% of population, reflects multi-SIM).
**Fixed broadband subscribers:** 550,000.
**Internet penetration:** 92.0% (October 2025).

## Competitive History (2024–2026)

**2024: Stable duopoly with entry signals**
- A1 Macedonia and Makedonski Telekom together held ~93% of mobile subscribers with no active competitive disruption.
- Makedonski Telekom had operated 5G since February 2022 across 26 cities (~82% population coverage) — first 5G mover in the market.
- AEK signalled intent to introduce a third mobile operator via a spectrum tender designed exclusively for new market entrants (existing operators excluded).
- Lycamobile operated as a small third carrier with ~6.6% market share, primarily on prepaid; no major commercial moves.

**2025: New entrant licence and regulatory overhaul**
- April 2025: AEK launched the 5G spectrum tender for new market entrants (Makedonski Telekom and A1 excluded). Only one bidder submitted — One Macedonia Telekomunikacii (subsidiary of Hungarian 4iG Group). AEK extended deadlines by 96 days at bidder's request.
- June 2025: Assembly of North Macedonia adopted the new Law on Electronic Communications, transposing the EU Electronic Communications Code and Gigabit Infrastructure Act. In force July 2025; applies from 1 June 2026. Key provisions: 1-day number portability at no charge, capped contract durations, simplified switching, infrastructure-sharing rules.
- September 2025: One Macedonia and Ericsson signed Letter of Intent to build one of the Western Balkans' first 5G Standalone networks.
- Mtel (Bosnia) finalized acquisition of Lycamobile North Macedonia operations (exact date not confirmed; Telecompaper reports finalization in 2025).
- Makedonski Telekom FY2025: Revenue +3.05% YoY; Q1 2026 customer growth across all segments. Capex €40M targeting 450,000 homes with gigabit connectivity and new Tier 3 data centre.
- ITU mobile basket price rose +33% YoY in 2025 — significant ARPU uplift not driven by a single operator announcement but structural repricing across the market.

**2026: Third operator launches**
- January 2026: AEK officially awarded the mobile licence to One Macedonia Telecommunications. Commercial launch expected in Skopje, initially using existing operators' networks (national roaming) while building own infrastructure.
- March 2026 (MWC Barcelona): One Macedonia and Ericsson signed full general contract for 5G SA network build. Target: first city 5G by end 2026, major transport corridors by 2028, all cities by 2030, nationwide by 2033.
- June 2026: New Electronic Communications Act fully in force — 1-day MNP, simplified consumer switching active.
- Makedonski Telekom Q1 2026: Mobile base +1.9% YoY to 1.28M (48.7% postpaid); fixed broadband +3.5% to 226,454; TV +6.1% to 171,428.

## Infrastructure State

**5G:**
- Makedonski Telekom: Live since February 2022; 26 cities, ~82% population coverage (2023 figures; no updated 2025–2026 coverage data found)
- A1 Macedonia: 5G commercially active; exact coverage not published
- One Macedonia: 5G SA network under construction with Ericsson; obligations — one city by end 2026, corridors by 2028, all cities by 2030
- North Macedonia ranks in top 5–10 in Europe for 5G speeds (~340 Mbps urban)

**Fixed:**
- Makedonski Telekom: Fixed incumbent; 550,000 fixed broadband subscribers nationally; targeting 450,000 homes with gigabit connectivity; new Tier 3 data centre planned
- National Operational Broadband Plan (2019–2029): 100% 100 Mbps availability by 2029, 1 Gbps for public institutions
- Fixed broadband household coverage: ~75.6% (2022 baseline)

## Regulatory History

**Key regulator:** AEK (Agency for Electronic Communications); competition authority: Commission for Protection of Competition (KZK).

**2022:** Makedonski Telekom launches 5G (first in market).

**2024:** AEK announces 5G spectrum tender for new entrants only; signals intent to break duopoly.

**2025:** AEK runs tender (April–September); One Macedonia wins; New Electronic Communications Act adopted (June 2025). Mtel acquires Lycamobile Macedonia.

**2026:** AEK grants licence to One Macedonia (January 2026). New Electronic Communications Act fully in force (June 2026). One Macedonia/Ericsson 5G general contract signed (MWC 2026).

## Key Tensions to Track

1. **One Macedonia commercial ramp** — Launching on national roaming initially; whether it can build subscribers before being forced onto own-network coverage is the central execution risk for 4iG
2. **Makedonski Telekom vs A1 response** — Both incumbents will face pressure to improve pricing and bundle offers once One Macedonia is commercially active; watch for promotional responses in H2 2026
3. **Mtel/Lycamobile positioning** — New Bosnian owner may relaunch Lycamobile as a price disruptor or fold it into a Balkan MVNO strategy; unclear commercial direction
4. **MNP activation under new act** — 1-day portability (effective June 2026) could accelerate switching flows if One Macedonia offers attractive switching incentives
5. **Makedonski Telekom capex execution** — €40M investment plan targeting gigabit coverage and data centre; success determines whether it can widen infrastructure gap before One Macedonia reaches network parity
