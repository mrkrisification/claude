# Slovenia Telecom Market — 2026 Pulse Log

## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
Slovenia's 2026 has been defined by the regulatory uncertainty hanging over Telemach's acquisition of T-2: the Competition Protection Agency opened a Phase II investigation on 16 March 2026, blocking the market from consolidating from four to three players until a verdict is reached. While that review runs, Telekom Slovenije has moved aggressively — cutting Naj plan prices and launching Slovenia's first 5G Standalone network — widening the gap against a Telemach that is operationally constrained by the merger process. A1 Slovenia decommissioned its 3G network to redeploy spectrum for 5G, signalling a similar infrastructure-first strategy.

### Last 90 days

- **M&A — Telemach/T-2 Phase II investigation** — Slovenia's Competition Protection Agency (AVK) opened an in-depth Phase II probe on 16 March 2026, citing "serious doubts as to compatibility with Slovenian competition rules"; outcome expected H2 2026 with no guaranteed clearance. This leaves Telemach operationally in limbo and prevents T-2 network integration, giving rivals a window. ([Concurrences](https://www.concurrences.com/en/bulletin/news-issues/preview/the-slovenian-competition-authority-opens-an-in-depth-phase-ii-investigation), 2026-03)

- **Infrastructure — Telekom Slovenije 5G SA launch** — Telekom Slovenije launched Slovenia's first 5G Standalone network with Ericsson (May–June 2026), targeting 99% population coverage; SA enables sub-10ms latency and network slicing, a commercial differentiation platform no competitor has matched. ([Ericsson press release](https://www.ericsson.com/en/press-releases/3/2026/telekom-slovenije-launches-5g-standalone-network), 2026-05)

- **Pricing — Telekom Slovenije Naj plan cuts** — All Naj mobile plans unified at €16.99/month (up to 800 GB, unlimited calls/SMS, 5G), dropping to €11.99 with a fixed-mobile bundle; locked for 24 months for new subscribers. This is a direct price-quality strike during the window when Telemach is M&A-constrained. ([alertify.eu](https://alertify.eu/telekom-slovenije-lowers-the-prices-of-the-most-popular-mobile-plans-in-the-biggest-5g-network-in-slovenia/), 2026)

- **Earnings — Telekom Slovenije Q1 2026** — Revenue €178.7M (+4% YoY), EBITDA €66M (+7%), net profit €16M (+12%); all metrics above consensus, signalling the incumbent is accelerating rather than defending. ([Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/telekom-slovenije-q1-net-profit-grows-12-to-eur-16-million--1571984), 2026-05)

- **Infrastructure — A1 Slovenia 3G shutdown** — A1 decommissioned its 3G network, reallocating spectrum to 5G; combined with the MIO unlimited plan lineup (200 GB and 500 GB throttle tiers), A1 is executing a quiet but consistent 5G upgrade path. ([SAMENA Daily](https://www.samenacouncil.org/samena_daily_news?news=95115), 2026)

- **Regulatory — EC wholesale broadband review** — The European Commission formally requested AKOS revise its wholesale high-quality broadband access market proposal; if AKOS loosens access terms, it would reduce Telekom Slovenije's fixed network advantage and benefit A1 and Telemach. ([EC Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-requests-slovenian-regulator-review-its-proposal-wholesale-high-quality-broadband-access), 2026)

**Directional verdict:** Intensifying — Telekom Slovenije is exploiting the Telemach/T-2 regulatory pause with simultaneous price cuts and a 5G SA launch, compressing the competitive window for all three rivals before the market potentially consolidates.

### Outlook to year-end

- **Telemach/T-2 Phase II verdict (expected H2 2026)** — A clearance would create a stronger third player capable of challenging Telekom Slovenije on converged bundles; a block would leave T-2 financially exposed and Telemach without its planned scale-up, likely pushing competition toward the easing direction in fixed.
- **AKOS wholesale broadband revision** — If the EC-requested revision loosens Telekom Slovenije's access pricing, A1 and Telemach gain fixed broadband cost parity; expected response H2 2026, but AKOS may delay beyond year-end.
- **5G SA competitive response** — Whether A1 or Telemach launches a 5G SA counter to Telekom Slovenije's May 2026 move will determine whether the incumbent's quality gap becomes permanent; no confirmed timelines from either challenger yet.
