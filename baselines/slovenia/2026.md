## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
2026 has opened under the shadow of the Telemach–T-2 merger review: after United Group's 2024 deal to acquire up to 98.06% of T-2, the Slovenian Competition Protection Agency escalated to a Phase II in-depth investigation in March, putting the market's medium-term structure (potential #2 mobile player with ~33% share, ~55% fixed broadband) in limbo. Meanwhile Telekom Slovenije closed out a strong FY2025 (revenue +4%, EBITDA +7%, beating all targets) and consolidated its mobile lead to 36.85%, while all four operators continued repricing — generally raising headline prices modestly but compensating with bigger data buckets and free streaming bundles.

### Last 90 days
- **Regulatory** — The Slovenian Competition Protection Agency (AVK) opened a Phase II in-depth probe into Telemach's bid for sole control of T-2 on 16 March 2026, citing serious doubts about market concentration. (Concurrences/Telecompaper, 2026-03-16) — this is the single biggest open variable for competitive structure and signals regulators see real risk of reduced competition.
- **Pricing/promotions** — A1 Slovenia is running a streaming-bundle promo (free Netflix/HBO Max/SkyShowtime/VOYO for 3 months on new MIO sign-ups) from 25 February to 31 December 2026. (a1.si, 2026-02-25) — content bundling rather than price is the main retail battleground.
- **Pricing/promotions** — Telemach launched a fresh discount campaign on its "VEČ" mobile packages (up to ~€17.70/month off on 24-month contracts) running 28 May–30 June 2026, available to new and existing customers. (telemach.si, 2026-05-28) — aggressive retention/acquisition push amid the pending merger uncertainty.
- **Pricing pressure** — Reporting (Lider.si, early 2026) flagged continued upward pressure on Slovenian telecom bills via indexation and package restructuring even where operators avoid headline price-hike announcements. (Lider.si, 2026) — underlying cost pressure persists despite promotional generosity.
- **Leadership** — Telekom Slovenije's supervisory board reappointed CEO Boštjan Košak for a new four-year term starting October 2026, citing a successful turnaround in market-share trajectory. (STA, 2025-12) — signals continuity in the incumbent's strategy of share recovery via repriced "Naj" plans.

**Directional verdict:** Intensifying — the pending Telemach/T-2 merger review has created structural uncertainty that is fueling aggressive promotional activity (streaming bundles, multi-year discounts) from all sides as operators jockey for position ahead of a potential market reshuffle.

### Outlook to year-end
- **AVK Phase II decision on Telemach/T-2** — timing uncertain (Phase II probes typically run several months from March 2026 opening); a clearance, block, or conditional remedy (e.g., spectrum/network divestitures) would be the defining event of 2026 for market structure.
- **AKOS 4GHz spectrum band procedures** — flagged in AKOS's 2024 annual report as expected to begin in 2025/2026; if launched, could reshape 5G capacity competition, particularly for smaller players like T-2.
- **Streaming-bundle promo expirations** — A1's free-streaming offer runs through 31 December 2026; whether rivals match or escalate this content-bundling approach as it nears expiry will signal whether the "bundle wars" are structural or promotional.
