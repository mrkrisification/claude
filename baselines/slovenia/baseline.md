# Slovenia Telecom Market — Structural Baseline

**Last updated:** 2026-06-09
**Covers:** 2024–2026 (structural history)

---

## Market Structure

Four mobile network operators, consolidating toward three:

| Operator | Owner | Mobile share (end-2025) | Fixed broadband share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Telekom Slovenije | Slovenian state (~62%) | ~36.9% | ~34% | Incumbent; largest fiber network (460k+ homes passed); only operator to grow profit in 2024 |
| A1 Slovenia | A1 Telekom Austria Group | ~25.5% | ~19% | Only declining A1 Group market in FY2024; EBITDA –7.3% YoY |
| Telemach | United Group | ~24.7% (brand only) | ~33% (rising to ~55% post-T2) | Dominant cable/FTTH; acquiring T-2 pending AVK clearance |
| T-2 | Garnol (being acquired by Telemach) | ~7% | ~22% | Financially distressed; independent operation suspended pending merger ruling |

**Mobile penetration:** ~133% (Q4 2025). **Market size:** ~€600M (2024).

**MVNOs:** HoT Mobil (on A1), HIP Mobil and Spar Mobile (on Telekom). All niche. No new entrants 2024–2025. Telekom discontinued "Izi" sub-brand August 2025.

---

## Competitive History (2023–2025)

**2023–mid-2024: Acute price war**
Unlimited data bundling drove margin compression across all operators. A1's "MIO" plans and Telemach's "VEČ" bundles established uncapped data as the baseline expectation. Three-way FTTH overbuild (Telekom SiOL + Telemach cable + T-2 fiber) in the same urban areas intensified discounting. T-2 faced insolvency.

**Mid-2024: Consolidation move**
Telemach (United Group) signed a binding agreement in August 2024 to acquire ≥98.06% of T-2 for ~€90M in debt assumption. Rationale: scale needed to remain competitive; three-way overbuild unsustainable. If approved, combined fixed broadband share ~55%; combined mobile share ~33%.

**Late 2024–2025: Attempted repricing**
Both A1 and Telemach announced coordinated price increases for January 2026 — a sign of the market trying to move from price war to oligopolistic repricing. Telekom had already raised prices in November 2024 (rivals initially held).

**2025: Telekom strengthens**
Telekom Slovenije FY2024: revenue +2%, EBITDA +5%, net profit +17%. Extended mobile leadership. Launched 5G Standalone network (Ericsson) in early 2026 — first in Slovenia, enabling network slicing and enterprise services.

## Infrastructure State

**5G:**
- Telekom: 99% population coverage (end-2025 target met); 5G SA launched early 2026
- A1: ~85% population; fastest 5G per Opensignal (204 Mbps avg); won Opensignal award 2024–2025
- Telemach: lower coverage breadth; highest peak throughput (302 Mbps); €51.6M spectrum spend; €100M investment committed

**Fiber/Fixed:**
- Telekom SiOL: 460k+ homes passed (>50% of country); up to 2 Gbps
- Telemach: ~350k homes (DOCSIS 3.1/XGS-PON); near-ubiquitous 1 Gbps
- T-2: symmetric gigabit FTTH; footprint to be absorbed by Telemach if merger approved
- National FTTP coverage: 76% of households (vs EU average 56%)

## Regulatory History

**2021:** Major multiband 5G auction — €164.2M raised; 700 MHz, 1.5 GHz, 2.1 GHz, 2.3 GHz, 3.6 GHz, 26 GHz assigned to all four operators.

**2023:** AKOS reduced wholesale access obligations for Telekom Slovenije (central and local access markets), citing reduced market power. A1 responded with a €33.7M damages lawsuit.

**2023–2024:** Regional/local 5G spectrum tender (2.3 GHz and 3.6 GHz bands) for municipalities and industrial use. As of October 2023, Slovenia had assigned >98% of harmonized 5G spectrum — highest in CEE.

**February 2024:** Slovenian Competition Act amendment — stricter merger notification rules. 40 of 45 competition decisions in 2024 concerned mergers.

**August 2024:** Telemach–T-2 merger announced; binding SPA signed.

**March 2026:** AVK (Slovenian Competition Authority) opened Phase II in-depth investigation into Telemach/T-2 merger. Found "serious doubts" across mobile, fixed telephony, broadband, and linear TV. T-2 characterized as "an important driver of competition."

**Pending:** EU Digital Networks Act (Commission proposal expected end-2025; Council guidance December 2024). Will reshape wholesale access obligations across all member states.

## Key Tensions to Track

1. **AVK verdict on Telemach/T-2** — the single biggest variable for market structure
2. **A1 Slovenia's trajectory** — only loss segment in a nine-country group; sustained pressure may force a strategic response (aggression, exit, or consolidation)
3. **Telekom privatization** — government periodically explores sale; Deutsche Telekom/Magyar Telekom cited as most likely buyer; no binding deal as of mid-2026
4. **PPF interest in Telemach** — Czech media group reportedly considering acquisition of Telemach; could complicate T-2 deal or introduce new ownership dynamic
5. **5G SA differentiation** — Telekom's head start in standalone 5G may shift competition axis toward enterprise/B2B
