# Slovenia Telecom Market — Baseline

*Last refreshed: 2026-06 (18-month lookback to ~late 2024)*

## Market structure

Four nationwide-relevant players:
- **Telekom Slovenije** — incumbent, state-influenced (majority owned via Slovenian Sovereign Holding/SDH-linked structures), market leader in mobile.
- **A1 Slovenia** — part of A1 Telekom Austria Group, positions itself as a "boutique" challenger with strong B2B/cloud/cybersecurity push. CEO Dejan Turk (also covers A1 Croatia, appointed June 2024).
- **Telemach Slovenia** — owned by United Group, broadband/pay-TV market leader (EON platform), pursuing convergent fixed+mobile bundling. CEO Adrian Ježina, who also now leads a combined Telemach Slovenia–Croatia business cluster.
- **T-2** — smaller, fixed-broadband/fiber-focused #4 mobile operator, historically state-creditor-entangled (Slovenian Sovereign Holding, SSH).

End-2025 mobile market shares (AKOS data, via Telecompaper): Telekom Slovenije 36.85%, A1 Slovenia 25.45%, Telemach 24.74%, T-2 the remainder. Telemach leads fixed broadband (~33% share). 5G now ~44% of mobile lines vs 4G ~51%.

## Defining structural event: Telemach–T-2 acquisition

The single dominant story shaping this market since mid-2024:
- August 2024: Telemach Slovenia (United Group) signed a binding agreement to acquire up to 98.06% of T-2 from Garnol, in two phases (initial 24.9% stake first), alongside an **€90m debt settlement** with Slovenian Sovereign Holding (SSH) — €37m paid immediately, €53m due by 31 July 2027.
- If completed, the combined Telemach+T-2 entity would hold roughly **~33% of mobile** (overtaking A1 to become #2 behind Telekom Slovenije) and **~55% of fixed broadband**.
- **16 March 2026**: the Slovenian Competition Protection Agency (AVK/SCPA) opened an **in-depth Phase II investigation**, citing serious doubts about compatibility with competition rules given an already concentrated market. Outcome is pending — this is the single most important open question for the market's competitive structure.
- United Group has already begun integrating operationally: Telemach Slovenia and Telemach Croatia were combined into one business cluster under Ježina, and SkyShowtime (United Group's streaming JV) launched on Telemach's EON platform in Slovenia from 1 December 2025.

## Pricing dynamics

- All four operators have been actively repricing in 2025–2026, generally trending toward **higher headline prices but bigger data allowances/bundled perks** rather than straightforward discounting.
- Telekom Slovenije: cut headline "Naj" mobile plan prices to €16.99 (€11.99 with fixed bundle), revamped plans with much larger data buckets (+45% network traffic growth cited), and ran 4-year price-guarantee promotions on gigabit 5G plans.
- A1 Slovenia: "MIO" mobile family (mini/midi/maksiMIO) with unlimited calls/SMS/data (throttled at 31GB/500GB tiers); aggressive streaming-bundle promos (Netflix/HBO Max/SkyShowtime/VOYO free for 3 months, Feb–Dec 2026).
- Telemach: raised some fixed package prices (+€3/month from Jan 2026) while increasing mobile data allowances (20GB→33GB on "VEČ"); running steep multi-year discount promos (up to ~€17.70/month off on 24-month contracts).
- T-2: implemented a broad ~€2/month average price increase across residential/business services from August 2025, citing inflation and network investment costs.
- Overall: data/internet services ~52% of MNO revenue; ARPU described as stagnant domestically with roaming revenue recovering on tourism. Aggressive promotional bundling (streaming content, large data) is the primary competitive lever, not headline price cuts.

## Infrastructure

- 5G: A1 targeting 98% population coverage by end-2025 (and switching off 3G to refarm spectrum); Telekom Slovenije upgraded 1,000+ base stations targeting 99% coverage by end-2025; Telemach was first to "true gigabit" 5G via 3.5GHz spectrum (paid €51.6m for frequencies, committed €100m investment).
- Fiber: Telekom Slovenije covers ~60% of households with fiber (5Gbps symmetrical, XGS-PON path to 10Gbps); T-2 has been consolidating via bolt-on ISP acquisitions (e.g., Telesat); A1 acquired smaller fiber ISP "Peters Teleurh."
- Government targets: 100Mbps for all households and gigabit for key public institutions by 2025; "Gigabit for All"/full 5G coverage by 2030.
- Data center / AI infrastructure: a national supercomputer data center (Maribor, ~€18m, completion mid-2026) tied to a €150m EU-backed "Slovenian AI Factory" project — an emerging adjacent infrastructure theme operators may look to serve via B2B/cloud arms.

## Financial trajectory

- Telekom Slovenije Group FY2025: revenue €740.5m (+4% YoY), EBITDA €256.8m (+7% YoY, 34.7% margin), net profit €60.6m (+10% YoY) — beat all 2025 targets. 2024-2028 plan targets EBITDA €280m / net profit €83m by end-2028. CEO Boštjan Košak reappointed for a new 4-year term from October 2026, citing a turnaround in market-share trajectory.
- A1 and Telemach/T-2 standalone Slovenia financials are not separately disclosed in detail; both report through parent groups (A1 Telekom Austria Group, United Group).

## What to watch (feeds into "Outlook" sections going forward)

1. **AVK Phase II ruling on Telemach/T-2** — the central swing factor for market structure (approval, blocking, or conditional remedies/divestitures).
2. Continued promotional intensity around streaming-bundle giveaways (Netflix/HBO Max/SkyShowtime/VOYO) as a non-price competitive lever.
3. 5G/fiber coverage races toward 2025-end targets feeding into 2026 marketing claims.
4. Any AKOS spectrum activity (4GHz band procedures flagged for 2025).
