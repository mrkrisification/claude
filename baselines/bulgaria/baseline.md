# Bulgaria Telecom Market — Structural Baseline

**Last updated:** 2026-06-09
**Covers:** 2024–2026 (structural history)

---

## Market Structure

Three mobile network operators; no MVNO has successfully launched to date.

| Operator | Owner | Mobile share (end-2024) | Revenue (FY2024) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Bulgaria | A1 Telekom Austria Group | ~36% (~2.9M subs) | ~EUR 780M (est.) | Network quality leader; fastest speeds (Opensignal Jan 2026); key EBITDA contributor to A1 Group |
| Vivacom | United Group | ~32% (~2.6M subs) | EUR 752M (H1 2025 annualised) | Leads on 5G population coverage (~92%); dominant in fixed/cable; aggressive M&A (Bulsatcom, Telnet, Networx) |
| Yettel Bulgaria | e& PPF Telecom Group (majority e&, formerly PPF) | ~31% (~2.3M subs) | EUR 536M (FY2024) | Highest claimed mobile revenue; wins Opensignal availability/reliability awards; fresh capital from e& acquisition Oct 2024 |

**Total mobile subscribers:** ~7.978M (end-2024, down 0.4% YoY — market saturation).
**Market size:** ~EUR 1.63B combined operator revenue (2024); projected USD 2.27B in 2025 growing to USD 2.72B by 2030 (CAGR ~3.7%).
**ARPU:** Historically near EU bottom — Bulgaria marketed as having "Europe's cheapest data." Coordinated 2025 price increases signal end of deep-discount era.

**MVNOs:** No operational MVNO as of 2026. A consortium of five fixed/TV providers (Nwt 1, Neterra, SKAT, Telekabel, Telecom) has been attempting to launch a joint MVNO since ~2018; wholesale access negotiations with MNOs remain unresolved. Neterra separately pursuing a regulatory dispute with CRC over 5G tender conditions. No new MNO entrants expected.

## Competitive History (2023–2026)

**2022–2023: Infrastructure consolidation — Vivacom/Bulsatcom**
United Group (Vivacom's parent) acquired Bulsatcom's telecom infrastructure assets in November 2022. A1 and Yettel jointly challenged the Bulgarian competition authority's clearance. The legal battle ran for over three years.

**2024: Ownership change at Yettel**
e& (UAE's Etisalat) completed a majority acquisition of PPF Telecom Group's CEE operations (Bulgaria, Serbia, Slovakia, Hungary) in October 2024. This injected fresh capital and technology capability into Yettel, rebalancing competitive dynamics against Vivacom's infrastructure scale.

**Early 2025: Coordinated price increases**
All three operators raised prices simultaneously — A1 and Yettel by ~9.5%; Vivacom by ~4.5–4.7% (2 BGN/month on mobile/internet). The CRC (Communications Regulation Commission) opened an investigation. This marks a structural shift: Bulgaria's era of deep price competition is ending, with operators moving toward inflation-linked annual increases.

**June 2025: State-backed fibre contracts**
Bulgaria's government signed BGN 433M (~EUR 221M) in fibre deployment contracts with A1, Vivacom, and CETIN (Yettel's network arm) to connect 200+ settlements in 140 municipalities, funded partly via EU Recovery and Resilience Facility. Symmetric allocation across all three operators limits differentiation on coverage.

**February 2026: Bulsatcom legal saga closes**
Bulgaria's Supreme Administrative Court issued a final, unappealable ruling confirming the Bulsatcom acquisition did not constitute a notifiable concentration. This ends three-plus years of litigation and cements Vivacom's quad-play infrastructure position. Vivacom subsequently completed bolt-on acquisitions of Telnet and Networx.

## Infrastructure State

**5G:**
- Vivacom: ~92% population coverage; NSA architecture; coverage breadth leader
- A1 Bulgaria: First mover; NSA with SA trials; fastest speeds per Opensignal Jan 2026 (168.1 Mbps download, 26ms latency); Overall Experience and 5G Experience award winner
- Yettel/CETIN: Densifying with e& backing; Opensignal Jan 2026 winner for Time on Network, 5G Availability, and Reliability Experience
- All three received 700 MHz and 800 MHz blocks enabling improved indoor and rural 5G coverage

**Fiber/Fixed:**
- Vivacom: "10GIGA" program — BGN 600M (~EUR 307M) investment targeting 1.8M addresses passed; aggressive overbuild of legacy copper and coax
- All three share BGN 433M state-funded rural fibre contracts (June 2025)
- EU RRF: ~EUR 270M subsidising 5G corridors and FTTH in underserved areas

**Total sector investment:** Exceeds EUR 327M annually; ~3x national industrial average. Operators collectively earmarked BGN 1.1B (~EUR 560M) for 5G infrastructure.

## Regulatory History

**2023:** 700 MHz and 800 MHz spectrum allocated to all three operators, enabling sub-GHz 5G.

**2023–2024:** 900 MHz refarming and 2100 MHz renewal tenders underway; Neterra pursuing regulatory dispute over 5G tender conditions.

**October 2024:** e& completes majority acquisition of PPF Telecom Group (Yettel Bulgaria). No regulatory block.

**February 2026:** Supreme Administrative Court final ruling — Bulsatcom acquisition stands; A1 and Yettel appeals exhausted.

**2026 (ongoing):** CRC SMP market analysis reviews under EU Electronic Communications Code. Potential tighter wholesale access remedies on fixed broadband.

**EU Gigabit Infrastructure Act:** Provisions applicable from 2026 requiring streamlined third-party access to physical infrastructure.

## Key Tensions to Track

1. **Vivacom's post-Bulsatcom position** — quad-play (mobile + fixed + TV + infrastructure) advantage now legally cemented; A1 and Yettel must respond on product or pricing
2. **e& investment cadence at Yettel** — fresh capital could fund aggressive pricing, network upgrades, or both; watch Yettel ARPU and capex trajectory
3. **CRC SMP review** — tighter wholesale access remedies would benefit A1 and Yettel at Vivacom's expense
4. **MVNO entry** — Neterra-led consortium stalled 7+ years; any regulatory breakthrough adds a fourth competitive force
5. **Price increase normalisation** — if coordinated ~9.5% annual increases become the norm, competition shifts from price to quality/bundle; watch for CRC enforcement action
6. **A1 Group portfolio role** — Bulgaria explicitly cited as key EBITDA contributor offsetting Austrian weakness; A1 has strong incentive to defend share
