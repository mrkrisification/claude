## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
All three operators opened 2026 in a coordinated price-rise posture — the second consecutive year of inflation-linked fee increases — while mobile subscriber counts continue a slow structural decline (−0.4% YoY to ~7.98M at end-2024). The dominant dynamic is revenue-over-volume: operators are accepting churn in exchange for higher ARPU, supported by bundled fixed-mobile-TV convergence and state-backed fibre capex. The structural event shaping the year is e&'s late-2024 majority acquisition of Yettel and CETIN, which injected fresh capital and raised the competitive stakes on 5G infrastructure spending.

### Last 90 days
- **Price increase (market-wide)** — Vivacom raised monthly mobile and internet fees by BGN 2 (VAT incl.) effective Feb 8 2025, following Yettel's earlier BGN 2–3 hike; A1 followed. Second round of increases signalled into 2026, normalising annual price uplift indexed to inflation. ([Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/224329/Bulgarian+Mobile+Users+Face+Price+Hike+as+A1,+Vivacom,+and+Yettel+Raise+Monthly+Fees), 2025)
- **Subscriber & revenue erosion** — Mobile subscriptions fell 0.4% in 2024; CRC attributes part of the revenue drop to lower EU-mandated wholesale voice termination rates rather than retail weakness — implies retail pricing power remains intact for now. ([Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/233561/Bulgaria%E2%80%99s+Mobile+Operators+See+Continued+Drop+in+Subscribers+and+Revenue), 2026)
- **M&A — Bulsatcom court cleared** — Bulgaria's Supreme Administrative Court confirmed in Feb 2026 that United Group (Vivacom owner) need not file for merger notification on the Bulsatcom infrastructure acquisition, overruling A1 and Yettel challenges; cements Vivacom's content and passive-infrastructure advantage. ([BroadbandTV News](https://www.broadbandtvnews.com/2026/02/20/bulgaria-court-confirms-united-groups-bulsatcom-infrastructure-deal-not-subject-to-merger-notification/), 2026-02-20)
- **A1 Group Q1 2026 earnings** — A1 Telekom Austria Group posted Q1 2026 group revenue +3.9% YoY and EBITDA +4.6%, with international markets (including Bulgaria, Croatia, Belarus) driving an 11.5% service revenue rise that offset an Austrian decline; net result +14.7%; free cash flow +53.5% on lower capex. Bulgaria explicitly cited as a key EBITDA contributor. Group reaffirmed 2026 guidance: 2–3% revenue growth, ~EUR 750M capex ex-spectrum. No Yettel or Vivacom standalone Q1 results published as of June 2026. ([Finanzwire / A1 Group, 2026-04-21](https://www.finanzwire.com/article/telekom-austria-ag-etr-a1-telekom-austria-group-reports-q1-2026-results-1LqYr1flBfn))
- **Fibre contracts (BGN 433M)** — A1, Vivacom, and CETIN won state-backed fibre deployment contracts worth BGN 433M — symmetric allocation means all three upgrade simultaneously, limiting coverage as a differentiator but raising the capex floor. ([Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/a1-vivacom-cetin-win-fibre-deployment-deals-worth-bgn-433-million-in-bulgaria--1539741), 2025)
- **Network quality — Vivacom leads** — Opensignal's January 2026 report places Vivacom ahead on overall mobile network experience, adding pressure on A1 and Yettel to respond with coverage claims or promotional pricing. ([Opensignal](https://insights.opensignal.com/reports/2026/01/bulgaria/mobile-network-experience), 2026-01)
- **Spectrum — 900/1800 MHz consolidation** — CRC assigned remaining unused 900 MHz and 1800 MHz assets to A1, Vivacom, and Yettel, completing the refarming of legacy 2G/3G spectrum into flexible-use 4G/5G licences. Symmetric allocation again; no competitive differentiation, but boosts indoor capacity across all three networks. ([Spectrum Tracker](https://www.spectrum-tracker.com/news/52), 2026)
- **MVNO — BABTO lobbying CRC for mandatory access** — Industry body BABTO (of which Neterra is a member) is pressing CRC to impose wholesale mobile access obligations on MNOs, with the argument that CRC could embed such conditions in any spectrum tender restart. Bulgaria remains the only EU market with zero operational MVNOs; regulatory pressure is building but no CRC decision is imminent. ([Neterra](https://neterra.net/news/neven-dilkov-bulgarian-regulator-must-restore-the-telecom-market-balance), 2026)
- **Sector investment outpaces economy** — Telecoms investment and employment are growing faster than the national average, signalling operators are not in retrenchment mode despite subscriber decline. ([Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/235842/Bulgaria%E2%80%99s+Telecoms+Drive+Investment+and+Jobs,+Outpacing+National+Average), 2026)

**Directional verdict:** Stable — coordinated price rises and symmetric fibre capex are suppressing head-to-head retail competition; the primary battleground has shifted to infrastructure quality and bundle depth rather than price.

### Outlook to year-end
- **Yettel 5G acceleration** — e&'s capital injection (closed late 2024) is expected to fund accelerated 5G standalone rollout through 2026; if Yettel closes the coverage gap on Vivacom, retail competition on network quality could re-intensify in H2 2026.
- **Second inflation-linked price round** — A further BGN 1–2 monthly fee increase across all operators is plausible in Q3 2026 given the established annual pattern; would sustain ARPU growth but risks CRC scrutiny if timing is again coordinated.
- **CRC wholesale access review** — Fibre programme runs to end-2026; any CRC decision imposing open-access obligations on the new fibre infrastructure could reshape MVNO and wholesale dynamics — direction and timing uncertain but would ease competitive barriers if imposed.
