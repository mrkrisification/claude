# Austria Telecom Market — 2026 Pulse Log

## 2026-06 — June

### Year so far
Austria's mobile market has been defined in 2026 by MVNO expansion squeezing MNO margins from below while the three main operators (A1, Magenta, Drei) compete for fixed-mobile bundle lock-in from above. A1 Group's domestic segment has seen revenue pressure — growth is being carried by its CEE subsidiaries — while Magenta and Drei have each committed multi-billion-euro infrastructure programs to defend share with network quality rather than price. No structural M&A has reshuffled the market; competition is primarily a tariff and ecosystem battle.

### Last 90 days

- **Earnings — A1 Group Q1 2026** — Group revenues rose 3.9% YoY driven by 11.5% international segment growth; the Austria domestic segment declined, with group mobile ARPU compressing to €7.5, signalling ongoing domestic pricing pressure. Net result surged 14.7% on lower CAPEX (€164m vs €222m prior year) and a 53.5% jump in free cash flow; 2026 guidance of 2–3% group revenue growth reaffirmed. ([EQS News](https://www.eqs-news.com/news/corporate-news/a1-telekom-austria-group-q1-2026-results/e25ad4bb-c22f-4a24-9dc1-df1d3a972747) / [Finanzwire](https://www.finanzwire.com/article/telekom-austria-ag-etr-a1-telekom-austria-group-reports-q1-2026-results-1LqYr1flBfn), May 2026)

- **Tariff competition — MVNO pressure** — Austria's MVNO segment reached 18.3% mobile market share; discount brands HoT and Spusu offer 50–60 GB plans at €9.90/month on Drei's wholesale network, undercutting premium post-paid bundles by more than two-thirds and eroding mid-market ARPU for all three MNOs. ([Mobile Europe](https://www.mobileeurope.co.uk/austrias-mvno-market-share-climbs-to-18-3/), 2026)

- **Infrastructure — Drei/oGIG fiber wholesale pact** — Hutchison Drei signed a wholesale fiber agreement with oGIG (Allianz subsidiary) extending fiber reach to 3 million homes, deepening Drei's fixed-line competitive capability without own-network CAPEX. ([Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/hutchison-drei-signs-fibre-optic-wholesale-pact-with-allianz-subsidiary-ogig--1468415), 2026)

- **Spectrum — April 2026 auction** — The TKK raised €39.41m from an auction of 2600 MHz renewal rights and a first-time allocation of 2300–2360 MHz (unpaired TDD block), completing the RTR Spectrum Release Plan 2022–2026 cycle. Per-operator breakdown: Magenta paid €13.18m for 100 MHz; Drei paid €15.21m for 100 MHz; A1 paid ~€11m for the remainder. No new entrants; this closes the 2022–2026 spectrum plan with all major bands now renewed. ([Heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/New-5G-frequencies-auctioned-in-Austria-11255680.html) / [Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/austria-raises-eur-39-mln-in-auction-of-23-26-ghz-bands--1567961), April 2026)

- **Infrastructure commitments** — Magenta reaffirmed a €2bn network investment to 2030 targeting 2.5m fiber premises (~60% of households); Drei is allocating €1.2bn for 5G expansion with 5G SA already covering 1.3m homes. Austria-wide FTTP household coverage stands at 38% (Q4 2024, Federal Ministry broadband evaluation Sep 2025), with Vienna and Tyrol leading at ~68%. 5G population coverage exceeds 90%. Both capex commitments signal competition shifting to infrastructure quality over near-term price cuts. - **Pricing — Magenta January 2026 tariff update** — Magenta published updated tariffs effective 8 January 2026; fixed internet entry tier (Internet S) set at €37.90/month. No headline price cuts; positioning focused on gigabit tier upsell. ([checkeverything.at](https://www.checkeverything.at/en/blog/internet-plans-austria-2026), April 2026)

**Directional verdict:** Intensifying — MVNO share gains are compressing MNO domestic ARPU while all three incumbents are locked into heavy capex commitments, reducing pricing flexibility and raising the cost of retaining subscribers.

### Outlook to year-end

- **A1 Austria segment recovery (Q2–Q3 results, August/November 2026)** — A1 must demonstrate domestic revenue stabilisation; failure to arrest the Austria decline while CEE carries group growth would increase investor pressure for domestic restructuring or a promotional price response, which could trigger a broader market response.
- **Magenta fiber rollout pace** — Magenta's gigabit fiber build (target: 2.5m premises by 2030) will produce H2 2026 coverage announcements that directly challenge A1's dominant >44% fixed broadband share; acceleration here is the clearest path to an Intensifying verdict in fixed.
- **MVNO share threshold** — If MVNO penetration breaks through 20%, RTR may face lobbying pressure for a wholesale access review; any tightening of wholesale terms would ease MNO margin pressure but is unlikely to be resolved before 2027.
