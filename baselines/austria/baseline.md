# Austria Telecom Market — Structural Baseline

**Last updated:** 2026-06-09
**Covers:** 2024–2026 (structural history)

---

## Market Structure

Three mobile network operators plus a large and growing MVNO segment.

| Operator | Owner | Mobile share (subscribers) | Revenue trend | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Telekom Austria | A1 Group (A1 Telekom Austria AG) | ~35% | Domestic declining; group growing via CEE | Market leader; fixed broadband dominant (>44% share); 9 CEE/SEE markets in group |
| Magenta Telekom | Deutsche Telekom | ~32% (RTR) / ~25% (commercial) | Slight decline in Q1 2026 | UPC Austria heritage in fixed; EUR 2bn network commitment to 2030 |
| Drei (Hutchison Austria) | CK Hutchison | ~20–24% | FY2025 revenue -4%; flat EBITDA | 5G SA pioneer; wholesale-only fixed strategy via oGIG/A1 |
| MVNOs | Various | **18.3%** (+1.5pp YoY in Q1 2025) | Fastest-growing segment | HoT (~9.6%), Spusu (~5.2%), Bob, yesss!, 42 total active MVNOs |

**Note on share data:** RTR regulatory data gives Magenta ~32% subscriber share; commercial research reports cite ~25%, likely reflecting revenue share. Use RTR figure for subscriber comparisons.

**Total mobile market:** ~USD 5.55B (2025), growing to ~USD 5.77B (2026), CAGR ~4%. Total mobile lines ~11M (including MVNOs).
**Austrian economy context:** GDP contracted ~0.1% in 2025; 2.9% inflation. Suppressing ARPU and accelerating value-plan migration.

## Competitive History (2024–2026)

**2024: Infrastructure M&A and spectrum**
- A1 acquired NTT Austria (enterprise digital services, April 2024) and Conexio (fibre infrastructure, September 2024) — bolt-on tuck-ins, no structural market change.
- RTR awarded 26 GHz and 3.6 GHz third 5G spectrum blocks (March 2024): total raised EUR 25M; Drei acquired 600 MHz of 26 GHz spectrum.
- RTR and BWB (Federal Competition Authority) held joint press conference "Is competition at risk?" — signalling regulatory concern about three-player concentration.

**2025: MVNO surge and MNO margin pressure**
- MVNO market share rose to 18.3% in Q1 2025 (+1.5pp YoY), driven by HoT (~1.4M subs) and Spusu (~700k subs). Austrian Post announced plans to launch a mobile + internet MVNO in Q2 2026 — most significant new entrant in years.
- A1 Group Q3 2025: Austria domestic described as "very challenging conditions" — the only A1 Group market with domestic revenue pressure; CEE and SEE carried group growth.
- Drei H1 2025: revenue +3%, EBITDA +6% — but FY2025 full-year revenue fell 4% as H2 deteriorated under competitive pressure.
- Magenta mobile communications: slight +1.2% growth; leveraging Deutsche Telekom's pan-European scale.
- Magenta network sharing deal with Drei: approved by RTR, details not public.

**2026: Spectrum renewal and infrastructure race**
- April 2026: RTR 2.3/2.6 GHz spectrum auction raised EUR 39.41M — Magenta 100 MHz (€13.18M), Drei 100 MHz (€15.21M), A1 ~75 MHz (€11M). Completes RTR Spectrum Release Plan 2022–2026. No new entrants.
- Magenta Telekom + Meridiam (French infrastructure investor): EUR 1bn FTTH joint venture targeting 650,000+ households and businesses in rural Austria by 2030.
- Drei signed wholesale fibre agreement with oGIG (Allianz subsidiary) covering 3 million homes — Drei pursues fixed without own CAPEX.
- A1 Group Q1 2026: domestic Austria declined; international (Bulgaria, Croatia, Belarus) carried 11.5% service revenue growth. Group EBITDA +4.6%, net result +14.7% on sharply lower CAPEX.
- All three MNOs raised prices 1 April 2026 via Wertsicherung (CPI-indexed) clauses: A1 +3.5%, Magenta +3.5%, Drei +6.5% (compounded from skipped 2025 round). Austrian law does not grant customers extraordinary cancellation rights for index-linked increases.

## Infrastructure State

**5G:**
- Population coverage: >90% across all three operators
- Drei: 5G SA (Standalone) live, covering 1.3M homes; markets sub-10ms latency
- A1: NSA with SA rollout in progress
- Magenta: EUR 2bn total commitment to 2030 including 5G densification
- All major spectrum bands now renewed through next licence cycle (post April 2026 auction)

**Fixed/FTTH:**
- National FTTP household coverage: 38% (Q4 2024, Federal Ministry evaluation Sep 2025); Vienna and Tyrol ~68%
- A1: dominant fixed incumbent; >44% fixed broadband market share; own nationwide fibre network (~67,000 km)
- Magenta: EUR 1bn FTTH JV with Meridiam targeting 2.5M premises by 2030
- Drei: wholesale-only fixed strategy via oGIG and A1 network access — no own fibre build

## Regulatory History

**Key regulator:** RTR (Rundfunk und Telekommunikations Regulierungs-GmbH); competition authority: BWB/AFCA.

**2022–2024:** RTR Spectrum Release Plan 2022–2026 executed in phases; 3.6 GHz and 26 GHz awards (2023–2024); 700 MHz, 1500 MHz previously awarded.

**2024:** RTR + BWB joint press conference questioning adequacy of three-player competition. Magenta-Drei network sharing approved.

**2025:** Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021 and RTR Telecom Network Security Regulation in force covering 5G vendor security.

**April 2026:** 2.3/2.6 GHz auction closes the RTR 2022–2026 spectrum cycle. No SMP wholesale access changes identified in public sources; Omdia Country Regulation Overview 2025 covers detail (paywalled).

**EU context:** EU Digital Networks Act (proposal 2025); EU Gigabit Infrastructure Act (2026). Austrian progress tracked under EC Digital Decade. FTTP target of 82% by 2025 (EU) not yet met at 38%.

## Key Tensions to Track

1. **A1 Austria domestic decline** — only major A1 Group market under revenue pressure; watch for domestic restructuring, promotional response, or enterprise pivot to ICT/digital services (target: 25% unregulated revenue by 2030)
2. **MVNO penetration trajectory** — at 18.3%; Austrian Post entry in Q2 2026 could push past 20%; RTR may face pressure for wholesale pricing review at that threshold
3. **Magenta FTTH acceleration** — EUR 1bn JV with Meridiam is the most aggressive fixed overbuild in years; challenges A1's dominant fixed share directly
4. **Drei revenue trajectory** — FY2025 revenue -4%; if decline continues in 2026, CK Hutchison parent may reassess Austria commitment or seek consolidation
5. **RTR/BWB competition review** — Joint "is competition at risk?" press conference in 2024 signals regulatory appetite for intervention; potential SMP remedy review in fixed or wholesale
6. **April 2026 Wertsicherung CPI increases** — customers cannot exit; reduces short-term churn pressure but concentrates competitive risk in MVNO segment attracting switchers
